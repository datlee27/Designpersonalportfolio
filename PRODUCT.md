# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, potential clients, and fellow developers evaluating technical and design capabilities.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio to showcase creative web development skills, projects, and professional experience. Success is measured by leaving a lasting, memorable impression that leads to outreach.

## Brand Personality
Overwhelmingly impressive, highly professional, bold, brutalist, and cinematic.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS templates, sterile white backgrounds, flat design, unopinionated or safe UI, cluttered dashboards.

## Design Principles
- **Show, don't tell**: Demonstrate technical skill through cinematic scroll interactions and layout rather than just writing about it.
- **Bold Confidence**: Unapologetic typography and high-contrast brutalist aesthetics.
- **Cinematic Pacing**: Treat the scroll as a narrative timeline, revealing information deliberately to create a sense of awe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Maintain high contrast (WCAG AA), ensure focus-visible states for keyboard navigation, and keep the scroll smooth but respectful of standard interactions.
